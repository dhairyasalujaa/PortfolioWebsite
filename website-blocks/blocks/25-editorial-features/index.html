Editor’s letter

Attention is a form of care.

This edition begins with the spaces we almost overlook: a shaded threshold, a worn tool, a room shaped around afternoon light.

Our contributors ask what changes when we stay longer. The answer is not nostalgia. It is a richer account of the present—one assembled through detail, patience, and conversation.

Elena Ward, editorLondon · September 2026
